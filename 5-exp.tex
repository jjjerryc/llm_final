\paragraph{Decision checkpoints.}
Before full-scale evaluation, we must establish that (i) the baseline classifier's outputs are reproducible, (ii) review-to-paper mapping and PDF extraction work on the chosen split, and (iii) input-format differences do not invalidate the proposed comparison. We will document the evaluated sample count, configuration, and any setup changes. Dense retrieval, additional classifier architectures, and extra datasets are outside the minimum scope.
